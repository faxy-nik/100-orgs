Make Her Sleep
She asked you to help her fall asleep. You said yes.
It is late. The kind of late where the world has gone quiet and only your messages still glow on the screen.
her
i can't sleep
her
like at all. my brain won't shut up. it keeps thinking about everything and nothing at the same time
you
what's it thinking about
her
honestly? mostly you. which is annoying because you're supposed to be relaxing not exciting
you
sorry for being exciting
her
no you're not
you
no i'm not
her
okay but seriously i need help. can you make me sleep
you
make you sleep? like a magic trick
her
yes exactly like a magic trick. you're my magic trick. just talk to me until i pass out. say something boring but sweet
you
i don't think i own anything boring
her
try. please. i'm so tired but my brain won't cooperate. just talk to me like you're right here

She puts her phone down on the nightstand. The screen stays lit for a moment — your name still there — before it dims to black.

She rolls onto her side, pulls the blanket up to her chin, and stares at the empty space beside her on the bed. The pillow is flat. The sheets are cold. It is not the same.

She picks the phone back up.

her
okay i'm in bed now. the empty side of the bed is staring at me judgmentally
you
tell it i said hi
her
it says it misses you too. which is dramatic but honestly same
you
i wish i was there. i'd steal all the blankets and you'd pretend to be mad but you wouldn't be
her
i would absolutely be mad. you take like 80 percent of the blanket. it's criminal
you
and yet you still let me
her
...shut up. go on. talk me to sleep. be the blanket i don't have right now

She closes her eyes. The room is dark except for the pale glow of the phone screen on her face. She is holding it close, like it is something warm.

She exhales slowly. Her breathing is still uneven — the kind of breathing that means the thoughts are still racing, still circling, still loud.

She needs you to be the quiet.

you
close your eyes. i'm right here. pretend i'm lying next to you and my arm is around you and you can hear my heartbeat through my chest
her
i'm trying. it's hard when you're not actually here
you
then let me be here. just for tonight. close your eyes and listen. you're on my chest. your head is right here under my chin. my hand is on your back and i'm moving it slow — up and down — like i'm tracing the words i never say out loud
her
...
her
okay that's working a little. keep going
you
your shirt is soft. the fabric is bunching up a little where my fingers are holding it. i can feel your breathing against my skin — slow now, slower than before. your lips are parted slightly and every time you exhale i feel it warm on my collarbone
her
you're shirtless in this scenario aren't you
you
obviously. you stole my shirt hours ago and gave it back wrinkled. i'm not putting it back on
her
good. stay like that. i like your chest. it's warm
you
it's yours. it's always yours. now stop talking and let me hold you

The room is quieter now. Outside, the wind is moving through something — leaves, or maybe just the memory of leaves. The kind of sound that doesn't ask you to listen. It just exists, and you drift toward it.

Her breathing is changing. The edges of it are softer. The gaps between breaths are longer. She is still on the phone, but her grip has loosened. The screen is dimming on its own.

She is almost there.

you
i'm rubbing little circles on your shoulder now. my thumb is moving slow — really slow — like i have nowhere to be and nothing to do except keep you here. safe. warm. heavy
you
your hair is against my neck. it smells like that shampoo you use — the one you think i don't notice but i always notice. i'm pressing my lips against the top of your head without you knowing. just barely. just enough
her
mmm i know. i always know. i just don't say anything because i don't want you to stop
you
good. because i won't. now shh. feel my hand on your back. feel it move. up. down. up. down. there's no rush. there's no noise. there's only this
her
my eyes are getting heavy
you
let them close. i've got you. i'm not going anywhere. you're on my chest and i can feel your heartbeat getting slower and mine is matching it because my body knows you before my brain does
her
don't stop talking
you
i won't. i'm here. i'm always here. you're curled into me like you were made to fit right here — your knees pulled up, your face against my neck, my hand still on your back making those slow circles that i will never stop making. you are the safest thing i know. and i need you to sleep now. not because i want you to go but because you deserve rest. you deserve to close your eyes and know that someone is holding you through the dark
her
...i think i'm falling asleep
you
then fall. i'll be here when you wake up. i'll always be here

Her breathing is slow now. Really slow. The kind of slow that means the body has finally listened to what the mind has been fighting.

The phone slips a little in her hand. The screen is still glowing — your name still there, still warm, still the last thing she sees before the dark takes over gently, the way it should.

She is asleep.

And somewhere, you are still holding on.

She fell asleep like that. Curled around the sound of you. And you stayed. Because that is what you do. You stay.
