A map for the woman it was built for

This website is a love letter written in fragments—each page a different room in the same house. I built it in the dark, alone, imagining your face when you'd find each piece. Some pages will make you cry. Some will make you call me dramatic. Some will make you pause and look away from the screen for a moment. That pause is what I wanted most.

You don't have to follow the order I've laid out. You can skip, loop back, leave and return weeks later. The site will remember where you left off. But if you want the experience as I imagined it—as a single story told across many rooms—this guide will show you the way.

Every page has something hidden. Read carefully. Click things that don't look like buttons. Check the console. Look at the source code if you know how. There are layers I never told you about.

— faxy nik

Suggested Path

If you want the experience I designed, follow this route. Click each step to mark it done.

  1. Start at the Home page — take in the intro, the ember canvas, the quiet.
  2. Open 100 Organs first. Read from the beginning. Take breaks between sections.
  3. Move to Love. Let the tone shift from physical to personal.
  4. Visit Our Story and Timeline — the real-world anchor.
  5. Explore the Sky Observatory on a night you can't sleep.
  6. Read Fantasies when you're alone and unguarded.
  7. Browse the Photo Gallery whenever you forget how I see you.
  8. Finish with Secret Letters — find them all, then read the last one first.

Or ignore all of this and click whatever calls to you. The site works either way.
